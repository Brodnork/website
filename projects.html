STOMP!

December 16 2016 - April 11 2019

The first big project I ever worked on, Stomp was a webcomic I drew as a teen. One day I realized the story was way too long and convoluted and not very good, so I cancelled it. A few years later I took a better version of the story and started turning it into a game!

One day I'm hoping to get an archive of the comic up, but I want to wait until the game is done.

The Kestral

2020 (unfinished)

The Kestral was a Minecraft mod I started working on back in 2020 that adds a desolate and lonely new dimension to explore full of strange and terrifying creatures. The project was inspired by the feelings that beta Minecraft gave me, exploring an empty and sometimes unnerving world.
