Who the hell is Bee

Hi! I'm Bee, and I have a tendency to just make whatever the hell I want.

I look exactly like this in real life.

Games tend to be one of my favourite mediums to work with, but the kinds of projects I make include short animations, weird video things, Minecraft mods, and a Skin Furby.

I've been making art and using my imagination for as long as I can remember - almost all of my earliest memories are of things I drew as a little kid. I was coming up with (very bad) ideas for video games probably around the same time I was learning how to read, and I drew comics all throughout school. I've always had big ambitious ideas bouncing around in my head. Making stuff just seems to be the most natural desire in the world to my brain! It's just what I do!

Why'd you make a website?

Every year I feel like I'm getting more and more tired of social media. I yearn for spaces I can make my own, places I can fill with love and decorate to my heart's content. I want somewhere I can host my projects and talk about what I want without worrying that some third party site is going to get bought out and slowly turned to shit.

I want somewhere that belongs to me and not some random company.

And also like I said I just like making whatever the hell I want! A website is a fun project to chip away at!
