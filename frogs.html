Behold my frogs

I have 5 pet frogs! 4 whites tree frogs, and one pacman frog. I love these goofy little creatures so much, and I have so many photos of them.

They're all kept in bioactive enclosures - tanks that are filled with soil, plants, beneficial fungus, and microfauna like isopods and springtails. The isopods eat the frog poop, and the springtails eat the isopod poop, and it all turns into dirt in the end! It's very enriching for the frogs to live in a more natural environment, and also it's just very nice not having to clean up their poop and change the substrate all the time.
